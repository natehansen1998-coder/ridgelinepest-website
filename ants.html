Common Ants in Washington County

Southern Utah's desert climate supports several ant species that commonly invade homes and businesses. Proper identification is key to effective treatment:

Pavement Ants

Small, dark brown ants that nest in cracks in pavement and foundations. Common kitchen invaders.

Sugar Ants

Small ants attracted to sweets and moisture. Often found near sinks, counters, and food sources.

Harvester Ants

Red to brown ants common in southern Utah yards and desert lots. Known for painful stings and large bare mounds.

Southern Fire Ants

Aggressive reddish ants that nest in sunny, open ground and sting when disturbed. Increasingly common across the St. George area.

Stinging Ant Warning

Fire ants and harvester ants deliver painful stings and will defend their mounds aggressively, a real hazard for kids and pets playing in the yard. If you spot mounds or bare dirt patches with heavy ant traffic, contact us for an inspection before someone gets stung.

What to Expect After Treatment

Here's what typically happens after our ant control service:

  • First 24-48 Hours - You may see increased ant activity as foragers carry bait back to the colony. This is normal and means the treatment is working.
  • Days 3-7 - Ant activity will significantly decrease as the colony is affected by the bait.
  • Week 2 - Most ant activity should be eliminated. The barrier treatment will continue protecting your home.
  • Ongoing - Our treatments provide residual protection for weeks. We back it up with our satisfaction guarantee.

Preventing Future Ant Problems

While our treatments provide excellent protection, here are some tips to help prevent ant problems:

Ant Prevention Tips:

  • Keep kitchen surfaces clean and free of crumbs
  • Store food in sealed containers
  • Fix leaky pipes and faucets (ants need water)
  • Seal cracks and gaps around windows, doors, and foundations
  • Keep tree branches and shrubs trimmed away from the house
  • Remove wood debris and mulch from touching the foundation
  • Address moisture issues in basements and crawl spaces
