Common Household Pests We Treat

Washington County homes face unique desert pest challenges year-round. Our technicians are experts at identifying and eliminating all types of household pests:

  • Ants - Including pavement ants, harvester ants, fire ants, and sugar ants
  • Spiders - From common house spiders to black widows and desert recluses
  • Scorpions - Bark scorpions and desert hairy scorpions found throughout the St. George area
  • Rodents - Mice, rats, and roof rats that can damage your home and spread disease
  • Wasps & Bees - Safe removal of stinging insects from your property
  • Cockroaches - Including the Turkestan and American roaches that thrive in our desert climate
  • Earwigs & Silverfish - Moisture-loving pests drawn to bathrooms and irrigated landscaping

Seasonal Pest Prevention

In Washington County's warm desert climate, pest pressure never fully stops. Our year-round protection plans keep your home defended through every season:

  • Spring - Ants, spiders, and scorpions become active early as desert temperatures climb
  • Summer - Extreme heat drives scorpions, roaches, and other pests indoors seeking water and shade
  • Monsoon Season (July-September) - Storm moisture triggers spikes in ant and roach activity
  • Winter - Mild St. George winters keep many pests active while rodents move indoors
